## # 👾 Hey, I'm Remmy-Anne Imani Jura

🎮 **Software Engineering Student | Game Developer | Founder | Creative Technologist**

I'm a Software Engineering student at **Zetech University** with a passion for **game development, interactive experiences, and creative technology**. I'm fascinated by the way code, art, storytelling, and design can come together to create worlds that people can explore, enjoy, and connect through.

🚀 My goal is to become a **game developer and creator of original games** that aren't afraid to experiment, tell meaningful stories, and *rewrite the rules of play.*

### 🎮 What I'm Into

* 🕹️ Game Development & Game Design
* 💻 Software Engineering
* 🌍 Interactive & Immersive Experiences
* 🎨 Creative Coding
* 📖 Storytelling through Games
* 🤝 Building inclusive gaming communities
* 🌱 Learning, experimenting & turning ideas into playable experiences

### 👑 Founder — Glitch & Grace

I'm also the founder of **Glitch & Grace**, a community built **by female gamers, for female gamers**.

> **"Rewriting the rules of play."**

Our mission is to redefine community in esports and casual gaming through **mentorship, safe competitive play, authentic empowerment, and meaningful connections**.

We believe gaming should be a space where women can **play boldly, compete confidently, create freely, and belong unapologetically.**

### 💡 My Vision

I'm building my skills one project at a time, exploring new technologies, and turning the ideas in my head into things people can actually play and experience.

**Code. Create. Play. Repeat. 🎮✨**

> *The best games don't just entertain you — they give you a world you want to return to.*
